## In short

Momo is a **commercial desktop product** that I designed, built, and ship on my own: a local-first writing app for novelists, available for **Windows (Microsoft Store), macOS, and Linux**, with a paying user base.

- **Role**: founder and sole engineer — product, UX/UI, desktop app, backend API, billing, website, and distribution.
- **Scale of the work**: version 1.10, 800+ commits, three codebases (desktop app, API, website).
- **Business model**: lifetime licenses per platform or for all platforms, plus a cloud-sync subscription, with a 30-day free trial.

## The problem

I'm also a writer — I've been working on novels for years under the pen name **C.H. Blacksmith** (<a href="https://chblacksmith.com.br" target="_blank">chblacksmith.com.br</a>). Every writing tool I tried was either too generic, too bloated, or required me to hand over my manuscripts to a cloud service.

Novelists need planning, writing, revision, and book layout in one place — and they need to trust that their manuscript stays theirs. So I built Momo, and it turned out other writers had the same problem.

## Origins

Momo is the result of years of iterating on the same idea:

- **2016** — Pena, my first experiments with writing tools
- **2019** — Feather Text Editor, a simple text editor for writers
- **2020–2021** — Feather and Apollo Writer
- **2022** — desktop experiments, the architecture, and the name: Momo
- **2023** — first implementation of Momo
- **2025** — full reimplementation and public launch

Each attempt taught me something about what writers actually need — and about what it takes to ship a product, not just a prototype.

## The product

Momo keeps the whole book in one project, from the first idea to the final EPUB. Everything is stored **locally, in an open file format**, works offline, and never feeds AI.

Main features:

- **Projects, characters, and timeline** — organize the story world next to the manuscript
- **Distraction-free editor** — focus mode, spell checker, keyboard shortcuts, and optional Vim mode
- **Revision system** — comments, suggestions, and multiple manuscript versions
- **Automatic backup** — versions saved every 5 minutes, restorable with one click
- **Task board, goals, and achievements** — keep the book moving even with little writing time
- **Narrative templates** — 3-Act, Hero's Journey, Save the Cat!, and custom project templates
- **Fountain screenwriting** — screenplays inside the same creative universe
- **Layout editor and export** — EPUB, DOCX, and PDF
- **Built-in documentation** in 3 languages

## Engineering

Momo is an ecosystem of three codebases that I designed and maintain:

**Desktop app** — **Tauri 2 (Rust)** for the native shell and **React + TypeScript + Vite** for the UI, styled with TailwindCSS. Tauri keeps the binary small and the app fast, while React handles the complex editor and project state. Distributed through the Microsoft Store and direct installers (macOS, AppImage, .deb, .rpm).

**Poseidon API** — **Django 5 + Django REST Framework** on **PostgreSQL**, running in Docker. It handles accounts, licensing and feature access, the free trial, payments with **Stripe** and **Mercado Pago** (including Pix), a partner program for authors, and release distribution. It was built to serve multiple Creative Olympus products, not just Momo.

**Website** — **React + TypeScript + TailwindCSS + Framer Motion**, with checkout, a customer area, and full **i18n** in English, Portuguese, and Spanish.

## Impact

Momo is live and sold to writers, who also use it to publish their books. It has been presented at **writing workshops and events**, and direct feedback from novelists keeps shaping the roadmap. Building it alone proved that shipping a real product takes much more than writing code: product decisions, design, infrastructure, payments, support, and distribution all have to work together.
